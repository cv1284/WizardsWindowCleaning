What we do

Windows and gutters are our bread and butter. Every property is different, so we quote each job individually.

Window cleaning

Interior and exterior cleaning of houses, flats (including 3-storey) and commercial buildings. Every window is done properly, frames and seals included. We use a water-fed pole system for hard-to-reach glass.

Gutter cleaning

Gutters cleared of moss, leaves and silt, with fascias cleaned to match. Blocked gutters cause damp and overflow, so a clear-out now saves bigger repair bills later.
